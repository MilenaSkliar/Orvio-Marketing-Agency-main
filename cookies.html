A lighter website

This website does not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other advertising trackers. References to these platforms describe services, not installed tracking.

Essential preference storage

We use localStorage under the key orvio-privacy to remember your acknowledgement of this notice. It stays in your browser until you clear it or reset your preference.

Form security

On PHP hosting the server may use short-lived session storage for anti-abuse rate limiting. This is used to protect the enquiry service, not to track advertising activity.

Manage your preference

Use Privacy preferences in the footer to show the notice again. You can also clear local site data in your browser settings.

External links

Links to third-party platforms open their services, whose own privacy and cookie policies apply.

Changes

If optional analytics or advertising tracking is added, this policy and the consent controls must be updated before those tools are enabled.

Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small values sent with website requests. Local storage keeps a preference in your browser. Both can support site features, but they work differently.

What this site remembers

The privacy acknowledgement is the only preference stored by the front end. Carousel positions, pricing tabs and selected form fields are not saved after you leave the page.

No advertising tags

Google and Meta graphics are local visual assets. Displaying them does not send browsing activity to Google Ads or Meta Pixel.

Local fonts and images

Fonts, illustrations and photographs are served with the website. The pages do not load remote font services or embedded social-media feeds.

Clearing stored data

Your browser’s site-data settings let you remove this website’s stored preference. Clearing it makes the privacy notice appear again on a future visit.

Essential session controls

If the PHP form is enabled, a session may limit repeated submissions. Disabling all cookies may affect that protection or the ability to submit an enquiry.

Future changes

Optional trackers should not be activated until the operator has reviewed the relevant requirements and provided appropriate information and choices.
